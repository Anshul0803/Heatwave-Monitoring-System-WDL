Sources & Stakeholders

Information architecture for the data and people involved in a climate early-warning workflow.

Data sources

  • Automated Weather Station (AWS) sensors
  • India Meteorological Department (IMD) datasets
  • Satellite and remote-sensing feeds
  • Localized observation reports

Forecast workflow

  1. Acquire observations
  2. Validate fields and station IDs
  3. Analyze temperature arrays
  4. Evaluate heatwave risk
  5. Publish advisory

Stakeholder roles

MET
Meteorologists

Review observations and forecast outputs.

DM
Disaster management

Coordinate warnings and response.

CIT
Citizens

Subscribe to region-specific advisories.
